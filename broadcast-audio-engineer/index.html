Broadcast audio engineering

Broadcast audio.
Sound beyond the room.

Jean-Pierre Boutros is a live, studio and broadcast sound engineer based in Lebanon. His documented broadcast work includes the Papal Mass at Beirut Waterfront during Pope Leo XIV’s 2025 visit.

Based in Lebanon. Available for professional audio projects worldwide, with international travel, dates and scope agreed for each engagement.

A broadcast brief has its own audience.

Broadcast audio serves the people listening through a transmission or programme, while FOH serves the audience at the venue. A production may allocate these to separate engineers. Jean-Pierre’s portfolio records his broadcast sound engineering for the Papal Mass at Beirut Waterfront.

For a new broadcast inquiry, identify the production format, schedule, transmission destination and the engineering position you need. Include the people responsible for the venue mix, monitors, recording and transmission so the boundaries between those teams are clear.

Live production and television context.

The existing professional record also lists television collaborations including Hek Menghanni on MTV Lebanon, Ghanni Al Aali on Abu Dhabi TV and Ethnophilia on Al Mayadeen TV. Together with the Papal Mass broadcast, these collaborations form the television and broadcast context of his professional practice.

The broadcast service sits alongside Jean-Pierre’s live and studio engineering work. Clients and production teams worldwide can make an inquiry, with the production location, travel, dates, required responsibilities and delivery requirements agreed directly.

Questions before the project.

Which project documents Jean-Pierre’s broadcast engineering?

The portfolio records his broadcast sound engineering for the Papal Mass at Beirut Waterfront during Pope Leo XIV’s 2025 visit to Lebanon, with a link to the official broadcast.

Is broadcast audio the same as the venue’s FOH mix?

They serve different listening contexts and can be separate assignments. Confirm whether the brief is for broadcast, FOH, recording or an agreed combination.

What television collaborations are listed?

Hek Menghanni on MTV Lebanon, Ghanni Al Aali on Abu Dhabi TV and Ethnophilia on Al Mayadeen TV are listed in the existing professional record. No additional programme-specific role is assumed.

Can international production teams contact him?

Yes. Send the production location, dates, technical brief and required engineering role. International availability and travel are agreed for the engagement.
