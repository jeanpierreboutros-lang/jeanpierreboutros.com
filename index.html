The engineer

An ear for the music.
A mind for the details.

I’m Jean-Pierre, a live and studio sound engineer based in Lebanon, working with artists, musicians, and production teams internationally.

My work moves between the stage, the studio, and the broadcast desk. I bring the same attention to each: understanding the performance and shaping the sound around it.

I founded StudioJPB in 2008. Alongside engineering and studio integration, I build audio software through Zynforge Audio, turning everyday production challenges into practical tools.
