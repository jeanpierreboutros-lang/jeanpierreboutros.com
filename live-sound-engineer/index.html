Live sound & FOH engineering

Live sound &
FOH engineering.

Jean-Pierre Boutros is a Lebanon-based international live sound engineer whose documented work spans front-of-house mixing, artist monitors, concert stages and festivals.

Based in Lebanon. Available for professional audio projects worldwide, with international travel, dates and scope agreed for each engagement.

The audience mix. The artist’s mix.

Front-of-house, or FOH, engineering shapes the mix heard by the audience. Monitor engineering serves the performer on stage. These are distinct responsibilities: Jean-Pierre’s portfolio identifies his actual role for each production, including FOH for Nancy Ajram at L’Olympia in Paris and monitor engineering for her performances in Riyadh, Jeddah and Dubai.

For a concert or festival inquiry, the useful starting point is the artist, the show format and the position you need filled. An artist-specific monitor assignment should be scoped separately from the festival’s overall audio production. The same distinction applies when a show has separate FOH, monitor, recording and broadcast teams.

Concerts and festivals, across different rooms.

The documented FOH credits include Al Shami and Abeer Nehme at Ehdeniyat International Festival in Lebanon, The Eighties… The Knockout at JACC in Kuwait, and Marcel Khalifé at the Royal Opera House Muscat. Each project record identifies the artist, engineering role, venue and date.

International concert, festival and touring inquiries are welcome. Share the schedule and technical brief so that availability, travel and the engineering assignment can be agreed before a booking.

Questions before the project.

What is the difference between FOH and monitor engineering?

FOH is the mix for the audience. Monitor engineering is the performer’s stage mix. Jean-Pierre has documented credits in both roles; the assignment is agreed for the particular show.

Can I book Jean-Pierre for a festival performance?

Yes. His festival credits include FOH for Abeer Nehme and Al Shami, and monitors for Adam, at Ehdeniyat 2025. Send the artist, dates, location and required position to discuss your production.

Is he available for international concerts or touring?

International inquiries are welcome from any country. Jean-Pierre is based in Lebanon; dates, travel and the scope of each concert or touring engagement are confirmed by arrangement.

Does a FOH booking include the PA system or a console?

Equipment supply is not implied by an engineering credit. Include your venue’s audio specification and equipment responsibilities in the inquiry so the required scope can be agreed.
