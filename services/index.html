Questions before the project.

Who is Jean-Pierre Boutros?

Jean-Pierre Boutros is a Lebanon-based international sound engineer working across live sound, FOH and monitors, recording, studio mixing, mastering, broadcast and audio system integration. He founded StudioJPB in Jounieh in 2008 and develops software through Zynforge Audio.

Is Jean-Pierre available for international projects?

Yes. Concert, festival, touring, recording, mixing, broadcast and studio integration inquiries are welcome worldwide. Availability, travel and the scope of each engagement are agreed directly.

What projects demonstrate his live and orchestral experience?

The portfolio includes FOH for Nancy Ajram at L’Olympia in Paris, Marcel Khalifé in Muscat, Abeer Nehme with the Qatar Philharmonic in Doha and the Lebanese National Philharmonic in Diman, plus artist-specific monitor assignments and festival performances.

How can an international client contact him?

Email jp@jeanpierreboutros.com or use WhatsApp at +961 3 883 863. Include the service, artist or production, location, dates and technical brief to discuss the project.
